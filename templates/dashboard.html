Dwell time on clicked products

Time between opening a product and coming back to the results. Very short visits (< 10 s) suggest the product was not what the user wanted; long ones suggest real interest.

Query length (terms)

Number of terms after pre-processing. Long queries are more likely to have no product with all the terms (AND), which is why the engine has a relaxed search.

Queries per mission

A mission is a sequence of queries of the same session with the same goal (they share a term). Missions with several queries are users refining a search that did not satisfy them.
